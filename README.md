About the MICROMar Atlas
The MICROMar Atlas is an interactive scientific platform developed to facilitate the visualization, exploration, comparison, and interpretation of spatial patterns of microplastic pollution in Brazilian coastal sediments.
The platform integrates data from the MICROMar Project, a large-scale standardized coastal survey of microplastic pollution in Brazil, translating a national dataset into an accessible and interactive environment for researchers, students, environmental managers, policymakers, and the general public.
Dataset coverage
Metric	Coverage
Georeferenced sediment samples	4,134
Beaches	1,024
Municipalities	211
Brazilian coastal states	17
Identified microplastic particles	24,639


What can be explored?
- Spatial distribution of microplastics along the Brazilian coastline
- Beach-level abundance and contamination indicators
- Microplastic typification, including morphotypes, polymers, and colors
- Comparisons among beaches, municipalities, states, and coastal regions
- Pollution load and ecological risk
- Quantity–risk relationships
- Environmental and anthropogenic factors associated with microplastic distribution
Main indicators
Indicator	Description
MPs/kg	Microplastic abundance in sediment
PLI	Pollution Load Index
PHI	Polymer Hazard Index
PERI	Potential Ecological Risk Index


Scientific background
The MICROMar Atlas is based on data generated by the MICROMar Project, funded by the Brazilian Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation and the National Council for Scientific and Technological Development (MCTI/CNPq, Process No. 409009/2022-9).
